How this works
  • For each of the 10,000 simulated projects, every risk is rolled like a dice. If its chance is 9%, it happens in about 9 of every 100 projects.
  • When a risk happens, its cost and delay are picked between your low and high values, and land most often near your most likely value.
  • Costs and delays are added up across all risks that happened. Delays add up one after another.
  • Yearly chances are scaled to your timeline, so a 32% chance per year becomes about 9% over 90 days.
